Vendored copy of the view3d browser structure/trajectory viewer
(view3d.html + js/3Dmol-min.js). Self-contained: 3Dmol.js is bundled locally,
no CDN. This is a snapshot of the canonical view3d viewer; refresh it from that
source if the viewer is updated upstream.
